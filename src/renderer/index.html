Ultra-Fast Modern Desktop Image & RAW/DNG Viewer

ImageView+ v1.2 is engineered for instant photo inspection. It extracts embedded high-resolution previews from DNG & camera RAW files in under 6 milliseconds, decodes WebP/AVIF on the GPU with zero copy, and pre-loads adjacent images in RAM for 0ms transitions.

1. Instant Folder Browsing & Fullscreen

Double-clicking any photo in Windows opens it immediately and indexes all other supported images in that folder. Press F, F11, or double-click the image for true borderless fullscreen.

2. Side-by-Side Comparison & 3× Loupe

Press C to split the screen and compare two photos with synchronized zoom and pan. Right-click any thumbnail in the bottom filmstrip to set it as the comparison image. Press L to activate the 3× magnifier lens.

3. Supported Image Formats (40+)

RAW & DNG: DNG, CR2, CR3, NEF, NRW, ARW, SRF, SR2, ORF, RW2, RAW, PEF, RAF, SRW, ERF, KDC, DCR, 3FR, IIQ, MEF, MRW, RWL, X3F
