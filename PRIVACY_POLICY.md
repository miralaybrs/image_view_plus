# Privacy Policy for ImageView+

**Last Updated:** October 23, 2026

**ImageView+** ("we", "our", or "the Application"), developed by **Hakan GERMAN / MARKA DESTEK** ([www.markadestek.com](https://www.markadestek.com)), is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application handles user data.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Local Processing
- **Local Operation:** ImageView+ operates **100% locally on your computer**. All image rendering, RAW/DNG preview decoding, format conversion, and EXIF metadata extraction occur solely in local memory on your device.
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers.
- **No File Uploads:** Your images, photos, and files are never uploaded to any remote server, cloud service, or third-party platform. Your media remains strictly on your device.

---

## 2. Application Preferences & Local Storage
- **Local Settings:** The Application stores non-personal UI settings (such as chosen theme, language preference, background style, window dimensions, and zoom behavior) locally on your PC within the standard local application data folder (`%APPDATA%\image-view-plus\settings_v12.json`).
- These settings are stored entirely offline on your local file system and are never transmitted across the network.

---

## 3. Telemetry and Analytics
- ImageView+ contains **no analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or telemetry scripts**.
- We do not monitor how often you open the application, what images you view, or how you interact with the software.

---

## 4. Internet Connectivity & External Links
- **Offline Functionality:** The Application does not require an active internet connection to function.
- **External Web Links:** The "About / Help" section includes an informational web link to our website ([www.markadestek.com](https://www.markadestek.com)). Clicking this link will open your default web browser outside of the application. Please refer to your browser's and the external website's respective privacy practices when navigating external sites.

---

## 5. Third-Party Libraries
The Application utilizes open-source libraries (such as Electron, Sharp / libvips, exifr) solely for user interface rendering and image decoding tasks locally on your device. None of these components collect or transmit personal user data in our application.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
ImageView+ does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the functionality of the software changes. Any updates will be reflected in the Application repository and associated release notes.
